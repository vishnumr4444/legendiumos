# User Guide

## Signing in
Your username + password from your admin. The theme toggle (top right) switches between Day and Night identities; your choice is remembered.

## Employees (Noel, Vishnu, Krishnaprasad, Vysakh)
- **Dashboard**: your open tasks, due dates, items in review, personal completion KPI, logged hours.
- **Board**: drag your cards between To do / In progress / In review / Blocked / Done. Click a card for details, files and comments.
- **Comments**: write naturally. If you're blocked or see a risk, say so; the AI flags it and your lead is notified instantly.
- You only ever see your own tasks. That's by design.

## Leads (Amal, Anson)
Everything above, plus:
- **Team Command dashboard**: pending approvals (items in review), blocked items, sprint progress, team workload.
- **AI Command**: decompose initiatives into ready-to-run plans for your team.
- **Workload**: utilization per member, one-click AI rebalances.
- **Reports**: scoped to your department.

## Status meanings
todo -> not started · in_progress -> being worked · review -> awaiting lead approval · blocked -> can't proceed (say why in a comment) · done -> complete
